Tickets

Real coalitions leak; 80–90% is typical. The default is 65%: bases of former rivals transfer poorly. The presidential candidate keeps all their supporters.

A candidate who backs the ticket without a place on it keeps fewer of their voters.

Where the others go

Voters who don't follow their candidate into a team. Shares are rescaled to add up to 100%. These are assumptions, not measured.

Opposition voters go to team A; team A's voters go to team B.
