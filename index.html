Article 138

The constitutional test. To win outright, a candidate needs 50%+1 of votes nationally and at least 25% in 24 of the 47 counties.

Joint Probability Surface P(nat >50%) AND P(counties ≥24) simultaneously
These conditions are correlated but not identical. A candidate can win nationally while failing the county test if their vote concentrates in 2–3 large counties without breadth. The joint probability models their simultaneous requirement.
